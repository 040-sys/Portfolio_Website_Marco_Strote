2. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you access the site, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data (including IP address, time of access, file requested, browser type) in server log files. Transfers to the USA are based on the Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Further information: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement

3. No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and embeds no analytics, advertising or social media services. There is no reach measurement and no profiling. Fonts are loaded exclusively from your operating system; no connection is made to Google Fonts or comparable services.

4. Contact by email

If you contact me by email, I process the data you provide (name, email address, content of your message) solely to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where a contractual or employment relationship is being initiated, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The data is deleted once the purpose ceases to apply and no statutory retention obligations prevent this.

5. External links

The website links to external profiles, in particular LinkedIn. Data is only transmitted to the respective provider once you click such a link. No social media plugins are embedded that would transmit data on page load.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
