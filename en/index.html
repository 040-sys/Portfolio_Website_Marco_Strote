04 — About

Two perspectives, one outcome

Consulting without delivery stays on paper. Delivery without consulting stays busywork.

I have spent several years working at the intersection of business, technology and management.

As a consultant I start with the question of which problem actually needs solving.

As a product owner I carry the consequences of those decisions all the way into delivery.

I am drawn to work where structure and people matter in equal measure.
